02 / ONCHAIN INTELLIGENCE

MOE AI SEES THE SETUP.

Moe AI is an explainable 4H market structure scanner designed to turn systematic trading logic into understandable, trackable market data. The scanner is the first layer of a larger AI-native system being developed around Base, with future learning, decision and agent-ready infrastructure.

01
SCAN Scan crypto markets for structured 4H setups using repeatable market structure rules.
02
EXPLAIN Show the structure, entry zone, invalidation, target and reasoning behind each setup.
03
LEARN Record signal history so results can be measured, reviewed and improved using real data.
04
EVOLVE Build toward decision engines, execution systems and Base-native AI agent infrastructure.

CREATOR-LED
