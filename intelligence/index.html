One ecosystem, different jobs.

Scanner studies structured market setups. Radar discovers and validates emerging tokens. Academy teaches the logic. Passport records identity and progress. Games create participation. The future Agent will execute only approved Scanner signals under strict limits.

01 • MOE SCANNER

Finds structured setups.

Scanner reviews market direction, displacement and retracement structure across multiple timeframes. It applies Moe’s large-candle and body-based Fibonacci rules, then reports entries, invalidation, targets, risk/reward, freshness and quality.

  • Higher-timeframe bias: 1D and 4H
  • Entry structure: 1H, 15m and selected 5m context
  • Ranks signals; future Agent accepts only quality ≥80
  • Produces information—not guaranteed trades
02 • TOKEN RADAR

Finds emerging attention.

Radar currently scans up to 12 trending Base pools and 12 Solana pools each hour. It scores liquidity, age, turnover, order flow, valuation and weekly structure. Potential alerts must pass contract-security and holder-intelligence validation.

  • Signal stages: Watch → Setup → Breakout
  • Hard security risks block alerts
  • Every qualified event is recorded permanently
  • 24h, 72h and 7d outcomes mature automatically
03 • ACADEMY + PASSPORT

Turns signals into understanding.

Academy explains the same structure, risk and validation concepts used by the products. Passport connects the holder’s wallet, NFT identity, completed learning, XP and participation into one evolving Moerverse profile.

04 • ARCADE + FLIP

Turns access into participation.

The games are lightweight holder experiences that add XP and repeat engagement. They do not influence Scanner or Radar rankings and cannot change market results.

How development learns.

1. ObserveCollect market and onchain facts without changing history.
2. RecordSave signal time, price, stage, risks, validation and delivery.
3. MeasureCalculate 24h, 72h, 7d, favorable move and drawdown.
4. ReviewImprove rules through versioned human review and testing.
Important distinction

Moe AI does not secretly rewrite its live rules after every result. Outcomes create an evidence set. Changes are reviewed, versioned and tested so past records remain comparable and weak ideas can be rejected.
