05 • MOE AI

The intelligence layer—not a generic chatbot.

Moe AI is the system that turns market and onchain observations into structured, reviewable information. Its value comes from Moe-owned rules, lifecycle logic, safety policies and accumulated evidence—not from placing an “AI” label over third-party data.

Now

Scanner produces versioned market setups. Radar performs validated token discovery. Holder dashboards and Discord distribute results. Outcomes are stored so performance can be measured.

Next

Increase evidence quality, mature immutable outcome records, measure false positives and drawdowns, improve research inputs only after validation, and connect education more directly to real system behavior.

DESTINATION & WHY

Trusted intelligence with accountable history.

The long-term advantage is not producing the most alerts. It is producing fewer explainable decisions, preserving what was known at alert time, and proving which rules remain useful across changing markets.

06 • MOE SCANNER v3.1

Structured trade setups from Moe’s method.

Scanner looks for large displacement candles breaking prior structure, uses candle bodies for Fibonacci pullback zones, combines higher-timeframe direction with lower-timeframe entries, and reports entry, invalidation, targets, risk/reward, alignment, freshness and quality.

Scanner converts a personal trading framework into consistent rules that can be tested. It does not remove uncertainty. The future Agent may accept only signals ranked 80 or above, but rank is a filter—not a promise of profit.

09 • MOER ACADEMY

Teach the reasoning behind the tools.

Academy v3 currently contains 24 modules across eight tracks, with lesson visuals, practice, knowledge checks and Passport progression. It covers structure, regimes, support/resistance, displacement, Fibonacci, invalidation, position sizing, expectancy, testing, journaling, Scanner logic, Radar context, Base security and controlled execution.

Current Academy

A usable holder curriculum tied to XP and titles. Lessons separate descriptive tools from guarantees and keep financial-advice boundaries visible.

Academy Intelligence Engine

A provenance-first research workflow is prepared as a review candidate: approved sources → quarantine → normalize → citations and deduplication → quality checks → draft → human approval → versioned publication. It is not autonomous publishing and is not yet fully integrated.

DESTINATION & WHY

A living curriculum with memory and accountability.

As Scanner and Radar mature, Academy can explain real patterns, real limitations and anonymized outcome lessons. Holders learn how to question signals—not merely consume them.
