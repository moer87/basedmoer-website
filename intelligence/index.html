6. Moe Scanner v3.1

Scanner converts Moe’s trading method into repeatable rules. It studies higher-timeframe direction, displacement candles breaking prior structure, candle-body Fibonacci pullbacks, lower-timeframe entry context, freshness, alignment and risk/reward.

1D / 4H direction → break + displacement → candle-body Fibonacci zone → 1H / 15m / 5m context → entry + SL + TP1–3 → waiting / open / won / lost / expired
Why: Consistent rules can be tested and improved. A strong rank is a filter, not a promise of profit.

7. Moe Token Radar v0.4

Radar currently scans up to 12 trending Base pools and 12 trending Solana pools per hourly cycle. Trending means current market attention—not safety or quality. Radar scores structure and tradeability, enriches stronger candidates, enforces validation and creates a permanent event when an alert qualifies.
